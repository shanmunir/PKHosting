What is billed monthly vs yearly?

Choose Monthly to pay each month. Annually charges 10 months for 12 and shows per-month equivalent.

Can I change plans later?

Yes — resize your VPS and billing adjusts on next invoice.

Do you support Pakistani payment methods?

We accept local bank transfers, JazzCash, Easypaisa, and major cards. See footer for choices.

Do these prices include tax?

Prices are listed without government taxes which may apply at checkout.
